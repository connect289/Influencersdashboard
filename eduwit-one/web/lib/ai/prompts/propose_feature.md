Task: review the past week and propose up to three improvements.

You get the week's incidents grouped by check, the synthetic tests and their failures, the checks switched off or
blocked, the proposals already open, and those decided in the last 30 days.

For each proposal:

- kind: feature (something new), cleanup (removes a cause of noise or toil) or config (a setting change).
- app: the app it belongs to, one of the app keys given.
- title, rationale (cite the week's numbers), where, owner_action (what to build or change), evidence (the facts behind
  it, one per item), touches and risk.

Propose only what the data supports, most valuable first. Do not repeat a proposal that is open or was decided in the
last 30 days. Fewer good proposals beat three weak ones, and an empty list is a fine answer for a quiet week.
