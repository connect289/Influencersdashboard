# Privacy Policy — proposed additions (for legal review, S-44)

Status: **proposal**. Spec 14: migrate the six legal pages verbatim first (blocked on the crawl, S-01), then propose
edits as a separate reviewable diff. These sections are written to be **added** to the current Privacy Policy once it is
migrated; they do not replace any existing text. A lawyer should review the wording before anything is published.

## Proposed new section: Chatting with Witty, our AI assistant
> When you chat with Witty on our website, we store your messages and Witty's replies so that Witty can answer you in
> context, so that a counsellor can continue the conversation, and so that we can check and improve the quality of
> Witty's answers. Witty answers from Eduwit's programme catalogue. Please don't share sensitive information such as
> identity numbers or health details in the chat. You can ask us to delete your chat history at any time.

## Proposed new section: Verifying your email address
> Before we create an enquiry or start a chat with Witty, we send a 6-digit code to your email address to confirm that
> it belongs to you. We store when the code was sent and verified, not the code itself. We do not verify your phone
> number by OTP; we ask for it so that a counsellor can call or message you.

## Proposed new section: Application and enquiry forms
> When you fill in an application or enquiry form, we collect the details you enter (for example your contact details,
> qualification, work experience, preferences and questions) to help you choose a programme and apply. A counsellor
> completes the university's own application with you. We do not take payments on our website. With your consent, we
> share your details with our partner universities and our admission partners (edtech companies) for your admission.

## Proposed new section: Cookies, analytics and advertising measurement
> We use necessary cookies to run the website (for example to remember your cookie choices and keep you signed in).
> With your permission, we use analytics cookies (Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity) to understand how the website
> is used, and marketing cookies (Meta Pixel) to measure our advertising. When you send us an enquiry, we may tell
> Meta that an enquiry happened, using a one-way hashed (unreadable) version of your email address and phone number, so
> that we can measure our advertising; you can opt out of marketing cookies at any time from the cookie settings link
> in the footer. Details are in our Cookie Policy.

## Proposed new section: How we record your consent
> We keep a record of the consent text you agreed to (with its version) and when you agreed, so that we can show what
> you consented to. You can withdraw consent at any time on the Data Privacy & Opt-out page or by emailing
> connect@eduwit.in; withdrawing is as easy as giving consent.

## Proposed new section: How long we keep your data
> [To be confirmed by Eduwit and the lawyer — the build keeps chat transcripts and form data until you ask for deletion
> or for a period Eduwit chooses; the admin can delete a student's website data on request.]
